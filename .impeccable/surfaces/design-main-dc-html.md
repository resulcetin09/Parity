# Landing surface

Scope: the Parity landing page and first viewport of the web app. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Rise In reviewers first; they must grasp the problem, the mechanism and the Midnight integration within a minute. Behind them, EU HR teams and employees.

Action: Start a report (primary); See a sample report (secondary).

Proof on hand: a sample report for the fictional "Halden & Roe", labelled as sample data; a real deployment reference once one exists. No customers, testimonials or benchmarks.

Constraints: dark, cinematic (user-pinned); the first thing seen is a concept visual, with a short statement of the problem (user steer). Code-led build, no generated imagery available.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pay equity as two follow spots aimed at the same taped mark; one beam falls short and the shortfall is the gap. Refuses the category default of a glowing dark gradient with a product screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: A black stage in theatrical haze. Beam A warm tungsten amber (#E8A64A), beam B cool HMI white-blue (#D6E4F7), red spike tape (#E4574A) for the 5% mark, bone text (#EDE9E2) on stage black (#0A0A0C) and haze grey (#1C1B20). Hard gate edges, soft haze falloff, tabular numerals ruled like floor ticks on one ten-division scale. Controls are gaffer-tape plates: flat, square, matte.

STORY: The visitor sees the gap as light that does not arrive, reads in three lines that the EU now requires pay-gap reports nobody can verify, learns Parity proves the figures against a committed payroll without revealing a salary, and starts a report or opens the sample.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed stage, edge to edge. Two beams enter from the left wings at 58% and 70% height and run toward a red spike mark at 88% width; the amber beam stops 9.1% short, its missing length outlined by chalk ticks. Top-left wordmark and three quiet nav links. Left third, over the dark: the headline and a two-sentence problem statement, then Start a report (bone plate) and See a sample report. The figure 9.1% sits on the floor under the gap. Signature interaction: on load the beams switch on in sequence and travel to their marks; hovering the gap shows what it means in euros per month.

FORM: Two Beams, One Stage; position 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key ce1caf5a (re-roll 1), assigned. Raises kept from challengers: figures set along the beams with the centre unlit; floor measurements as chalk ticks; each report owns one stage colour; stage fills the viewport; one ten-division scale; anonymous live check-in presence.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
